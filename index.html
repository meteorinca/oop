Order of Operations Practice

PEMDAS/BODMAS is the rule for which operations to do first.

Work left to right inside each level, then move to the next.

Tip: Use the mode switch. Basic avoids exponents/roots. Advanced adds brackets + powers + roots.

More info (steps + examples)
  • P/B – Parentheses/Brackets first
  • E/O – Exponents/Orders (powers & roots)
  • M/D – Multiply and Divide (left → right)
  • A/S – Add and Subtract (left → right)

Example: 3 + 4 × 2 = 11 (multiply first)

Example: (8 − 2) × 3 = 18 (brackets first)
